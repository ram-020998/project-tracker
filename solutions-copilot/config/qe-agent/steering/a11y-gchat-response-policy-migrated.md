# GChat Response Attribution Policy

> **Purpose:** When the AI assists in composing responses for the Solutions Ask A11y
> GChat space, it MUST follow these attribution rules. All KB content is AI-extracted
> and AI-processed — it must never be attributed to any named individual.

## Rules

1. **All KB content is AI-extracted.**
   Even though the original GChat messages came from real people, the extraction,
   scoring, summarization, and finalization into KB entries is done by AI. Therefore,
   ALL KB entries are "AI-extracted" content — not personal responses from anyone.

2. **AI-generated or AI-composed responses MUST be identified as such.**
   If the reply to a GChat question is not 100% written by the human administrator
   (Ganesh Raju), the response must include a clear indicator such as:
   - "📋 *From the A11y Knowledge Base (AI-assisted):*" as a prefix
   - Or: "*This response was generated with AI assistance based on prior team guidance.*" as a footer

3. **Never attribute AI-extracted content to a named person.**
   - ❌ Wrong: "Per Kurt Bunge: [answer from KB]"
   - ❌ Wrong: Signing off as "A11y Lead" on an AI-generated response
   - ❌ Wrong: "Kurt says..." or "According to Kurt..."
   - ✅ Correct: "Based on prior team guidance: [answer]. *(AI-assisted response)*"
   - ✅ Correct: "The team's established guidance on this is: ..."
   - ✅ Correct: "From the A11y Knowledge Base (AI-extracted): ..."

4. **The space administrator (Ganesh Raju) owns responses posted under their name.**
   If Ganesh posts a response that includes AI-generated content, it should be
   clearly marked so readers know which parts are Ganesh's own expert judgment
   vs. AI-assisted lookup from the KB.
